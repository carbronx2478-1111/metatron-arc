# Metatron Arc

AI-assisted public-source OSINT reporting and decision-triage workflow.

**Niche:** Structured public intel for small teams and individuals who can't afford Recorded Future-class platforms.

## What it does
- Public-source research reports (news, CISA, FBI advisories, MITRE, KEV, open IOC feeds)
- Decision trees: cost, forecast, outcome, regret
- Threat triage desk
- Daily freshness checks on public threat feeds

## What it doesn't do
- No classified feeds
- No private records
- No hacking tools
- Public sources only

## Status
Active development. Non-profit, free reports.
